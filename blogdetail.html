2023 Social Media Algorithms: What You Need to Know

June 15, 2023 • 8 min read

Social media platforms are constantly evolving their algorithms to improve user experience. Understanding these changes is crucial for businesses looking to maintain visibility and engagement.

Understanding the Algorithm Changes

In 2023, major platforms like Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok have shifted their focus toward authentic content and meaningful interactions. This means:

  • Original content is prioritized over reposts
  • Engagement metrics now value saves and shares more than likes
  • Video content continues to dominate, especially short-form

Key Strategies for 2023

1. Focus on Authenticity

Platforms are rewarding genuine, behind-the-scenes content that shows the human side of your brand. Consider:

Pro Tip:

User-generated content performs exceptionally well under the new algorithms. Encourage your customers to share their experiences with your brand.

2. Optimize Your Posting Schedule

With the emphasis on fresh content, timing is more important than ever. Our research shows:

Platform Best Posting Times
Instagram Weekdays 9-11 AM
Facebook Weekdays 12-3 PM
TikTok Evenings 7-10 PM

Conclusion

Adapting to algorithm changes requires flexibility and a willingness to experiment. By focusing on authentic content, optimizing your posting schedule, and leveraging new features, you can maintain strong visibility in 2023.
